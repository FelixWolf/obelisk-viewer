--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Second Life viewer artwork included in this program
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

assets/textures/alpha_gradient_2d.j2c
    From the Second Life viewer (skins/default/textures/alpha_gradient_2d.j2c).
    Included unmodified. The program decodes it at startup and uses it as the
    terrain layer blend ramp, flipping the rows to the orientation the Second
    Life viewer samples it in.

assets/character/avatar_skeleton.xml, avatar_lad.xml
assets/character/avatar_head.llm, avatar_upper_body.llm, avatar_lower_body.llm,
    avatar_eyelashes.llm, avatar_hair.llm, avatar_skirt.llm, avatar_eye.llm
    From the Second Life viewer (newview/character). Included unmodified:
    the default avatar's skeleton, appearance definitions and body meshes.

assets/ui/icons/notecard.svg, object.svg, script.svg, settings.svg,
    snapshot.svg, sound.svg, texture.svg
    Adapted from the Second Life viewer's inventory icons
    (skins/default/textures/icons/Inv_Notecard.png, Inv_Object.png,
    Inv_Script.png, Inv_Settings.png, Inv_Snapshot.png, Inv_Sound.png,
    Inv_Texture.png). Changed: redrawn as vector images (SVG), so they can be
    drawn sharp at any size.
